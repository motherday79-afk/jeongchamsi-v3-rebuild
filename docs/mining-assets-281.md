# Mining character parts 281

Built-in imagegen was used for three character part atlases and one adult arm atlas. Source prompts requested transparent, isolated, complete parts in a fixed cell grid, polished mobile-game rendering, warm upper-left lighting and a right-facing three-quarter view.

- Strong: existing brown work outfit, head, dressed torso, dressed legs, arms, lamp helmet, brown and navy clothing, gloves and boots.
- Glamour: clearly adult woman retaining existing work outfit; head, dressed torso and legs, separate work equipment, brown and purple clothing.
- Elf: cream long-sleeved pajamas and socks as non-removable base, cute head, brown and forest clothing, miner accessories.
- Arms: neutral adult male and female puppet upper-arm and forearm/gripping-hand pieces, no clothing or accessories.

Sprites were split and alpha-trimmed into `assets/mine/rig-281/`. Existing four pick illustrations were trimmed for use as complete held tools. Source sheet processing is recorded in the workspace `design/prepare-mine-281.cjs`.

Adult underwear base attempts were rejected by image generation and are not present. No generated adult underwear result is claimed. Adult torso/leg removal remains unavailable.
